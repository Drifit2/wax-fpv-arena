Creator checklist
  1. Choose and control a unique WAX collection account.
  2. Prepare artwork and pin it to IPFS.
  3. Create the AtomicAssets collection and compatible schema/template through a supported wallet-authorized workflow.
  4. Verify the on-chain template ID, immutable data, supply limits, and creator permissions.
  5. Mint through the official supported creator workflow, then test in-game ownership validation.
  6. List collectibles on AtomicHub only after reviewing marketplace fees, royalties, and terms.
Game entitlement integration requires signed wallet authentication and a server allowlist of approved collection/template IDs. Public account lookups alone are insufficient.
